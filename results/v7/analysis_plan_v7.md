## 2. Definitions

Competitive nulls, both judged by FDR inside the hit set:

- set-specific: the size-matched random-gene null has FDR < 0.10
- weight-specific: both nulls have FDR < 0.10. This is the existing module-specific call.

Two gate systems:

- program-level pass: robust and IPF-specific and set-specific
- clock-direction pass: robust and IPF-specific and weight-specific. This is the existing "통과" call.

Final categories: the six categories from instruction 6, applied once for each system. In the program-level system, set-specific occupies the place that module-specific occupies in the instruction 6 table.

| category | clock-direction system | program-level system |
|---|---|---|
| 통과 | robust, IPF-specific, weight-specific | robust, IPF-specific, set-specific |
| 견고·IPF 특이·비특이 | robust, IPF-specific, not weight-specific | robust, IPF-specific, not set-specific |
| 견고·모듈 특이·공유 | robust, weight-specific, not IPF-specific | robust, set-specific, not IPF-specific |
| 견고만 | robust, and neither of the two rows above | robust, and neither of the two rows above |
| hit만 | hit, and not robust | hit, and not robust |
| 탈락 | not a hit | not a hit |

Technical robustness: the coefficient ratio versus the r5 primary coefficient is at least 0.5 and the sign agrees, in all three of the following:

- technical covariates
- depth, group-matched
- cell count, group-matched

A stored ratio is the sensitivity coefficient divided by the primary coefficient. A ratio of at least 0.5 already requires the same sign. A missing ratio does not count as robust.

## 7. Pre-specified criteria

Every result is reported. Criteria are not revised after the results are seen.

Part A:

| item | criterion |
|---|---|
| P1 | among S4 pairs, the program-level pass rate is at most 1% |
| P2 | among S2a hits, the set-specific rate is at most 10% |
| P3 | among S3 target pairs, the IPF-specific rate is at most 10% (confirmation) |
| P4 | report the program-level MDE80 for S1b (no threshold) |
| S2b | no judgment. Report the relation between PC1 alignment and the set-specific rate |

Judgment: if P1, P2, and P3 all hold, the program-level definition is adoptable. If any one fails, it is not adoptable, and the failed item is named.

P2 and P3 use the pooled replicates and the pooled values of d. Rates at each d are also reported. The judged number is the pooled rate. set-specific includes hits that pass the random-gene null whether or not they also pass the coefficient-permutation null.

Part C, mortality composite only. For the mouse, beta is the old-minus-young coefficient. For the human, adjusted Spearman rho replaces beta. Human recovery in the "tissue stronger" row is the adjusted rho itself, because there is no rodent expected shift in rho units: tissue is stronger when the whole-donor adjusted rho is at least twice the median of the cell-type adjusted rhos and the whole-donor p is below 0.05.

| explanation | condition |
|---|---|
| tissue stronger | (ii) recovery at least twice the median of the (i) cell-type recoveries, and (ii) p < 0.05 |
| (a) composition | tissue is stronger, and the (iv) composition-only beta is at least 0.5 times the (ii) beta |
| (b) detection | (iii) beta is at most 0.5 times the (ii) beta |
| (c) absence | neither (a) nor (b) |

(a) and (b) may both hold. All ratios are reported. The mouse and the human are judged separately. Inequalities are applied as written, including when a coefficient is negative. Chronological scores and the 23 modules are reported and are not judged.

## Part A procedure

The 1,050 files in results/v6/spikein/parts/ already store p_comp_random, p_comp_perm, fdr_comp_random, and fdr_comp_perm. No replicate is rerun.

A non-hit has empty competitive FDRs. It is not set-specific and not weight-specific. weight-specific must match the stored module_specific flag; any mismatch is counted and reported.

Null-combination populations:

- S1a, S1b, S3: all target pairs
- S2a, S2b, S4: all hits
- S1a, S1b, S2a, S2b, S3: hits that are not targets, as a separate population

The four combinations are random-gene only, coefficient-permutation only, both, and neither.

Category rates are reported for both systems, overall and by cell type, for each scenario and d. Proportions also carry Wilson intervals. The intervals are descriptive. Criteria use the point estimates.

MDE80 uses the program-level pass rate. The interpolation is the instruction 6 rule: the smallest d in {0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0} at which the pass rate reaches 0.8, with linear interpolation between adjacent d values; "> 2.0" if 0.8 is never reached; the grid point 0.5 if the rate is already at least 0.8 there. A cell type with a missing rate at any d has a blank MDE. The clock-direction MDE80 is stored in the same file for comparison. S1b exclusion rows, if any, stay out of the denominator.

S2b alignment is the stored column alignment = sum(c · v_pc1) / ||c||. The report relates its absolute value to the set-specific rate among hits.

## Part C procedure

Expected shift. From Data_annotation_relative_rodents.xlsx, keep mouse rows with no intervention. Regress each training target on the age difference in months, with an intercept. Targets are Difference.Hazard.log10 and Difference.Chronological_age.Normalized_by_species_max_lifespan. Report n, Pearson r, and the slope per month. The Angelidis contrast is 24 minus 3 = 21 months. Expected shift = slope × 21. The column used to mark "no intervention" and the age-difference column are recorded in expected_shift_v7.csv. If a required column is absent, that regression is skipped and the reason is recorded.

Mouse units, Angelidis GSE124872, same preprocess as instruction 6 (species mouse, gene symbols, young 3-month median).

- (i) reuse results/v6/tms/composite_by_celltype_v6.csv and module_effects_v6.csv
- (ii) sum every cell within a mouse, then score
- (iii) same matrix as (ii), except a gene whose raw count is zero across the three testable cell-type pseudobulks (type II, alveolar macrophage, ciliated; at least 50 cells) is set to that clock's training median before scoring

For each unit and each score, report beta, the classical 95% interval, and the classical p from ols_full on score ~ old. Recovery = beta / expected shift. Coefficient mass detected = sum of absolute coefficients on genes with a nonzero raw count in that unit, divided by the sum of absolute coefficients.
