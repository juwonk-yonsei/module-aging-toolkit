## 2. Masking definition

Component Delta_k: the 23 modules and the one outside-module component, 24 components. Their sum is the composite effect.

- S = sum of |Delta_k| over all 24 components
- N = |sum of Delta_k| = |composite effect|
- MI = (S - N) / S

## 5. Contribution interpretation

Judged on the mortality composite, separately for mouse and human.

| judgment | condition |
|---|---|
| 분산됨 | the largest single cell type's contribution ratio is below 50%, and at least 3 cell types have a contribution ratio of at least 10% |
| 집중됨 | one cell type's contribution ratio is at least 50% |
| 중간 | neither of the two rows |

The contribution ratio is signed: beta_c divided by the expression-only beta. "Largest" is the maximum signed ratio. A ratio of at least 10% or at least 50% uses that signed value, so a negative ratio does not count. If the additivity ratio (sum of beta_c divided by the expression-only beta) is below 0.5 or above 2, the judgment is marked "가법성 낮음". Results are reported as they fall.

## Part C procedure

Mouse, Angelidis, same counts and preprocess as instruction 7. Expression-only is rebuilt with the instruction 7 rule (overall mean fractions, mouse-specific cell-type expression, young median centering) and is the denominator of the contribution ratio. For each annotated cell type c:

- R_c: cell type c uses the mouse-specific expression; every other cell type uses the all-mouse mean expression; fractions are the all-mouse mean fractions.
- R_-c: cell type c uses the all-mouse mean expression; every other cell type uses the mouse-specific expression; fractions are the all-mouse mean fractions.

A mouse with zero cells of type c uses the all-mouse mean expression for c, and the row records that imputation. Score the mortality and chronological composites. beta, classical 95% interval, and classical p come from ols_full on score ~ old. Contribution ratio = beta_c / expression-only beta. Leave-one-out effect = 1 - beta_-c / expression-only beta. Mean fraction is the all-mouse mean cell-type fraction. The standalone pseudobulk beta is copied from results/v7/resolution/mouse_units_v7.csv for the three testable cell types and is blank otherwise. Additivity ratio = sum of beta_c / expression-only beta.

Human, HLCA 77 donors, same pseudobulk as instruction 7. The expression-only reconstruction is scored again. The outcome is the OLS slope of the standardized composite on age in years, with sex, log library size, and the study prefix from instruction 7 as covariates. Scores are standardized across donors within that reconstruction (mean 0, sd 1) before the regression. The same regression is used for R_c, R_-c, and the standalone cell-type pseudobulk. Standalone slopes are reported for cell types with at least 12 donors and are blank otherwise. The judgment uses the mortality composite.

## Part D procedure

Targets: Macrophage_Alveolar / green, and Macrophage / grey60.

Direction, written to candidate_direction_v8.csv. On the saved GSE136831 preprocessed matrix for that cell type:

- official clock IPF coefficient from results/v3/human/r5/s9a_fdr_r5.csv
- unweighted score: the mean of the module's genes on that matrix, then the same IPF + age + sex contrast
